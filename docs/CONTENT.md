# Writing Batna content

The full rules are in `/mnt/project-files/medstudy/SPLIT.md` §4–5. In short:

- Courses: `content/program.yaml` lists the MSc's 20 courses (hours, group, semester, default status). A course owns every lecture whose id starts with its id: `hb.L01` is Hepatology, `res.L01` Basics of Research. `validate` fails on a lecture with no course. A course with `source: slides` (Basics of Research) is taught from slide decks: its `pages` are `[1, slides]` and `src.page` is the slide of that lecture; the app shows "سلايد N" and opens that lecture's slide. Slide text lives in `/mnt/project-files/medstudy/courses/<course>/text/`, never in git.
- There is one file per lecture: `content/<course>/LNN.yaml` (`content/hb/L01.yaml`), listed in `content/index.yaml` with its printed page range.
- Every item has `id` (`hb.L01.001`, never reused), `kind`, `topic`, `src.page` (inside the lecture's range), and `status: unverified`.
- The kinds are `card`, `cloze`, `mcq`, `enumerate`, `essay`, `case`, `table`, `order` and `calc`. `fixtures/content/sample.yaml` shows one of each.
- An `enumerate` item may carry a `hook`: a تحشيشة in Egyptian Arabic (medical terms in English) that makes the whole list stick: initials, a grouping with a count ("3 + 4 = 7"), a story or a pun. It shows under the points once they are open, in lessons and in review. It must name exactly the item's points and add no new facts.
- **A topic that comes back brings its تحشيشة back** (Mohamed, 2026-11-08). When a lesson step teaches a list that already has a تحشيشة, its `remind:` list names it, and the app shows it under the step: an enumerate item id (`hb.L01.033`, any course) or a mnemonic step as `<lesson id>:<WORD>` (`hb.L01.lesson2:SHIBS`). If it was taught in another lesson, the box says «من درس …» and opens that lesson. Give a repeated list the same تحشيشة everywhere rather than a new one; when the points only partly overlap (ACLF precipitants vs SHIBS), remind of the old one anyway. `remind` goes on explain, figure, aside, mnemonic or open-table steps only (never under a question: it would give the answer). `validate` checks each entry points at a real hook. Details in `docs/LESSONS.md` rule 13.
- Write from the book only. Put guideline differences in `update: { text, ref }`.
- `src.page` is the page that holds the fact the item tests (the answer, not the review-question page), and `src.quote` (required: every page chip shows it) is that page's own words containing it. There is no length cap (Mohamed, 2026-10-29): the lesson is the simple version and the quote is the primary source, so quote enough to prove the fact and give the full reference, e.g. the whole table or list, as text with one row or cause per line and sub-items indented. Never an image. `/mnt/project-files/medstudy/pagerefs/pick_quotes.py` proposes one and `set_quote.py` writes it.
- Page references are locked per lecture in `content/book-refs/LNN.tsv`. After changing a page or quote, run `cargo run -p batna-tools -- refs --lecture hb.LNN --write` where the book text is (`/mnt/project-files/medstudy/book`); a reference it can't confirm from the text gets read by eye and listed in `pagerefs/verdicts/LNN.tsv` (`ref<TAB>page<TAB>ok<TAB>reason`). `validate` fails on any page or quote that changed since its check, and on a quote that shares too few terms with its item.
- Calculator results cite a book page too, and their page chip shows `content/calc-quotes.yaml`: one quote per calculator and page its results cite, in the item-quote layout (tables as `|` rows, two spaces to nest). The app test `every_cited_page_has_its_book_quote` fails when a calculator cites a page without one. The quotes are checked and locked with the lecture's references as `calc:<calculator>@<page>` (`refs --lecture <id> --write`); one read off a page image needs a `quote-ok` line in the course's verdicts file.
- In an authored MCQ the key must not stand out as the longest option (#816): make a distractor as long as the key, or trim the key, without making the distractor less wrong. `batna-tools cue` shows how often the key is the longest option per lecture; a test fails if the bank share rises above the ceiling in `crates/tools/src/cue.rs`. Options printed in a source (`book-question`, `slide-question`, `past-paper`, `lecture-quiz`) stay as printed.
- Run `just validate` before every PR. It checks the schema, unknown keys (typos), ids, page ranges, MCQ shape, cloze syntax, table grids, calculators, and that nothing is `approved` unless Mohamed approved it.
